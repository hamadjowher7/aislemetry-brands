A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

A few good questions.

Who carries out the audits?+

Your promoters, field staff or agency partners. Aislemetry gives them a consistent audit and gives your brand a shared view of the evidence they collect.

What can we learn from a store audit?+

Focus on observable conditions: priority SKU availability, front-facing counts, category display share, branding, promotional execution and price communication. The question determines the evidence and the report.

How does this work alongside sales data?+

Store evidence adds context to what your sales and inventory systems tell you. A shelf photograph can show visible presence or an execution issue; sales performance and its causes need the relevant transaction, inventory or other supporting data.

What is the best way to get started?+

Start with one brand question, a product scope and the people you already have in the field. Book a call and we’ll work through a focused first audit with you.
